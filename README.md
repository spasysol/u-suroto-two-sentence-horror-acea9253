A horror story in exactly two sentences (217 characters):

> The lighthouse log says the light went out for nine minutes the night my father drowned, and no one ever found out why. I found his last entry this morning: 'The light never went out — something stood in front of it.'

The first sentence is a cold official record — a light that "went out" the night a keeper drowned. The second sentence re-reads it: the light never failed at all. Something tall enough to stand in front of a lighthouse lamp was out there in the dark with him, and the reader is left to picture how big it had to be. That is the whole entry: `main.py` writes this story to `/output/entry.txt`, nothing else.
